# Claims Policy

## Allowed evidence language

Use bounded states:

- unsupported
- plausible
- evidentially open
- provisionally supported
- replicated
- contradicted
- unresolved

## Never infer automatically

- altered state → anomalous information
- anomalous information → additional agent
- additional-agent fit → a specific entity
- specific claimed entity → extraterrestrial/interdimensional origin
- spiritual usefulness → physical mechanism
- statistical significance → practical or ontological significance

## Public result format

### WHAT WE OBSERVED
Directly recorded evidence.

### WHAT WAS CLAIMED
Source assertions, preserving exact wording.

### WHAT WE TESTED
Protocol and preregistered question.

### WHAT WE FOUND
Measured result and uncertainty.

### WHAT IT MAY MEAN
Bounded interpretations and alternatives.

### WHAT WE DO NOT KNOW
Open mechanism, identity, replication, and generalizability questions.
