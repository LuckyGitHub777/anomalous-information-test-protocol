# Roadmap

## v1.1 release objective

Close the v1.0 adversarial findings without expanding scientific scope:

- truthful claims/anchor assurance;
- protocol-enforced sealed writes;
- ledger verifier hardening;
- dry-run-only guided Laboratory until formal anchor UX earns promotion;
- accessible keyboard/focus/error behavior;
- 320/390 mobile reflow and reachable navigation;
- canonical Prediction Ledger freeze + adjudication;
- rendered browser UAT in CI;
- hosted Pages/readback and human claims/UAT sign-off.

## After v1.1 only if evidence justifies it

- ordinary 10–20 trial operator UAT;
- independently reviewed formal-anchor usability;
- custom target-pool selection/export for formal studies;
- study export/import bundles;
- effect-size and uncertainty visualization;
- research-ethics/consent templates after appropriate review;
- independent replication package;
- localization only if demand exists.

## Explicit holds

- cloud-hosted real experiments;
- accounts/social features;
- public participant data;
- AI scoring in the evidence-critical path;
- public anomalous-result leaderboards;
- source/ontology claims beyond evidence.
